Whether you're planning an intimate elopement for two or a grand celebration with hundreds of guests, Joy Inc. is here to make your wedding day everything you've dreamed of — and more. As a Connecticut-based wedding coordination company, we handle the details so you can be fully present for every moment that matters. From our budget-friendly **Elopement Essentials** all the way up to the **Ultimate Celebration**, our thoughtfully designed packages are built to give every couple the joyful, stress-free experience they deserve.

## How to Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse Our Packages">
    Use our package guide  to explore your options and find the coordination tier that best fits the size, style, and budget of your celebration. Not sure where to start? Our package estimator can help point you in the right direction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book a Free Consultation">
    Once you have a package in mind, reach out to schedule a complimentary consultation with our team. We'll take the time to learn about your vision, answer your questions, and make sure Joy Inc. is the right fit for your special day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize Your Coordination Plan">
    After your consultation and booking, we'll work together to lock in all the details — timelines, vendor coordination, day-of logistics, and everything in between — so your plan is perfectly tailored to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enjoy Your Wedding Day">
    On the day itself, your Joy Inc. coordinator handles it all behind the scenes. All you need to do is show up, be present, and enjoy every single moment of your celebration.
  </Step>
</Steps>
