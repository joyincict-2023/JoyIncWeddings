## Best For

The Intimate Keepsake is the right choice for small or intimate weddings — typically those with roughly 40 or fewer guests — where the celebration is close-knit but still deserves seamless, professional coordination. It's ideal for couples who want the peace of mind that comes from a coordinator who already knows their vision before the wedding day begins.

If you've done most of the planning yourself but want a dedicated professional to help you refine the details and then own the day-of execution, this package gives you that support without overcomplicating things.

## Events Covered

Your coordinator can manage any combination of the following events within your coverage window:

- First look and pre-ceremony photos
- Ceremony
- Cocktail hour
- Reception
- Dinner and formalities
- Send-off

Your timeline will be shaped around the moments that are most important to you.
