The Intimate Keepsake package is designed for couples hosting a small or intimate wedding who want more than just day-of coverage. Before your wedding, you'll sit down with your coordinator for a dedicated planning meeting so that on the day itself, they already know your wedding inside and out.

## What's Included

- Extended day-of coverage
- Pre-wedding planning meeting
- Final details review
- Custom day-of timeline, actively managed
- Vendor communication before and during the wedding
- Full on-site coordination of your selected events
- Optional rehearsal attendance

## Best For

Small or intimate weddings, typically around 40 or fewer guests, where the celebration is close-knit but still deserves seamless, professional coordination.
