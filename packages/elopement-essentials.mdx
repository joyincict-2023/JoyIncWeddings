## Best For

This package is the perfect fit if you're planning an elopement, a micro-wedding, or an intimate celebration with a small guest count and a simple, meaningful flow. It's especially well-suited for couples who have taken care of most of the planning themselves and simply want a skilled, calm professional to execute on the day — someone who will quietly manage the details while you focus on each other.

If your wedding is just the two of you, a handful of close family members, or a small group of your favorite people, the Elopement Essentials package gives you exactly the right level of support.
