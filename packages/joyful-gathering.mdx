The Joyful Gathering is built for traditional weddings with a full guest list, multiple vendors, and an event that flows from ceremony through reception and beyond. Your coordinator handles the details so you can be fully present for every part of your celebration.

## What's Included

- Full wedding-day coverage
- Multiple pre-wedding planning conversations
- Vendor coordination and confirmations
- Detailed day-of timeline shared with your vendors and wedding party
- Rehearsal attendance and direction
- Full on-site coordination from setup through send-off
- Wedding party and family liaison

## Best For

Traditional weddings with a full guest list, a ceremony-to-reception flow, and four or more vendors. Ideal if your day involves a venue, caterer, photographer, DJ or band, and florist working together.
