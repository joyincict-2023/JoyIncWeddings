## Best For

The Joyful Gathering is the ideal package for traditional weddings — celebrations with a full guest list, a ceremony-to-reception flow, and four or more vendors involved. It's designed for couples who want a coordinator who has already built a relationship with their team, aligned on the details, and is ready to lead on the day.

If your wedding has a venue, a caterer, a photographer, a DJ or band, and a florist — or any combination of the above — this package gives you the coordination depth to bring it all together smoothly.
