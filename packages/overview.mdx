## How to Choose Your Package

The right package comes down to the size of your wedding, the number of vendors involved, and how much support you'd like leading up to the big day. Smaller, self-planned celebrations often do beautifully with focused day-of coverage, while larger weddings with multiple vendors and a full ceremony-to-reception flow benefit from deeper coordination and more planning conversations.

Not sure where you fall? That's completely normal — most couples aren't.
