Working with Joy Inc. is designed to be straightforward and reassuring from start to finish. From the moment you reach out to the last dance of the evening, your coordinator is with you every step of the way — handling the details, managing the people, and making sure your day unfolds exactly as you envisioned.

## Your Journey With Joy Inc.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Package">
    Start by browsing Joy Inc.'s four coordination tiers to find the level of support that matches your wedding size, timeline, and needs. From the lightweight coverage of The Elopement Essentials to the full-day, two-coordinator experience of The Ultimate Celebration, there's a package built for your celebration. Visit the [packages overview](/packages/overview) to compare options and find your fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book a Free Consultation">
    Once you have a package in mind, reach out to Joy Inc. at **joyincict@gmail.com** to schedule a complimentary consultation call. This is your chance to walk your coordinator through your vision, share your venue details, discuss your vendor list, and talk through your ideal timeline. There's no obligation — just an honest conversation about how Joy Inc. can support your day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize Your Plan">
    As your wedding date approaches, your coordinator works closely with you to build a comprehensive day-of timeline. You'll confirm vendor arrival windows, go over ceremony and reception flow, review every logistical detail, and make sure nothing is left to chance. The goal is that by the time your wedding week arrives, every decision has already been made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attend Your Rehearsal">
    If your package includes rehearsal direction — or if your ceremony calls for one — your coordinator leads the walk-through. They'll guide your wedding party through processional order, cue timing, and positioning so that everyone feels confident and calm before the big day. You show up, follow along, and let your coordinator do the directing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enjoy Your Wedding Day">
    This is the moment everything has been building toward. Your coordinator arrives ahead of your guests to greet vendors, confirm setups, and get everything into place. Throughout the day, they manage the timeline, handle any unexpected hiccups, and serve as the go-to contact for your venue, vendors, and wedding party — so you never have to. Your only job is to be present and enjoy every moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-Wedding Wrap-Up">
    As the celebration winds down, your coordinator ensures all vendor departures go smoothly, overseeing breakdown and making sure nothing gets left behind. A brief debrief confirms that everything has wrapped up as planned, so you can leave for your send-off without a second thought.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Expect From Your Coordinator

Your Joy Inc. coordinator is more than a day-of manager — they're your advocate, problem-solver, and calm presence throughout the entire process. Here's what they handle so you don't have to:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Timeline Management">
    Your coordinator builds and owns the master day-of timeline, distributing it to all vendors and keeping every moving part on schedule from the first arrival to the final departure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Vendor Communication">
    On your wedding day, your coordinator is the single point of contact for every vendor — photographers, caterers, florists, DJs, and beyond. You won't be fielding calls or answering questions; your coordinator handles all of it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Point of Contact for Wedding Party & Guests">
    Your family and wedding party will always know who to turn to with questions, directions, or last-minute needs. Your coordinator keeps everyone informed and in the right place at the right time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Problem-Solving & On-the-Spot Decisions">
    No matter how carefully a wedding is planned, surprises happen. Your coordinator is experienced at staying calm under pressure, thinking on their feet, and resolving issues quickly — often before you even know there was a problem.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Setup & Breakdown Oversight">
    From ensuring the ceremony space is arranged exactly as planned to confirming that the reception is broken down properly at the end of the night, your coordinator oversees the physical flow of your event space throughout the day.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  For the best availability and the smoothest planning experience, we recommend reaching out to Joy Inc. at least **2–3 months before your wedding date**. Popular dates fill quickly, and earlier contact gives your coordinator more time to get to know your vision and prepare a plan that's truly tailored to you.
</Tip>
