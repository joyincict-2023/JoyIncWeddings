Joy Inc. is a Connecticut-based wedding coordination company dedicated to making your celebration everything you imagined — without the stress. From intimate elopements to large, multi-vendor receptions, our professional coordinators manage the moving pieces so you can focus on what truly matters: being present with the people you love.

## What Joy Inc. Does

Planning a wedding involves an enormous number of decisions, timelines, and people all converging on a single day. Joy Inc. exists to hold all of that together for you. Your dedicated coordinator steps in to manage the full scope of day-of logistics, from the first vendor arrival in the morning to the final send-off at night.

In the weeks leading up to your wedding, your coordinator works with you to build a detailed timeline, confirm vendor contacts, and ensure every arrangement is in place. On the day itself, they serve as the single point of contact for your vendors, venue, and wedding party — so no one is asking you where the florist should set up or when the photographer needs to arrive.

Whether you need light pre-wedding support for a quiet elopement or multiple planning sessions and a second coordinator for a large celebration, Joy Inc. tailors its services to fit your needs. Our goal is simple: every couple deserves to be fully present and genuinely joyful on their wedding day.
