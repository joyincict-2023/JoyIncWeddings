Joy Inc. is a Christ-centered wedding coordination company that has been serving couples since 2023. Everyone deserves someone in their corner on their wedding day, someone who understands your vision and brings every detail to life with care and intention.

## What We Do

With a Joy Inc. wedding coordinator, you get to dream, plan, and celebrate the moments that matter most while we keep everything running seamlessly behind the scenes. From managing the timeline to handling the unexpected, we'll orchestrate each moment so you can be fully present for every unforgettable memory.

**You planned the wedding. We'll protect the experience.**

## Meet Allison Joy

Hi. I'm Allison Joy, the owner and coordinator of Joy Inc. I've loved being a part of the wedding industry for the past 15 years. I've done everything from catering to hair and makeup, but my favorite job is wedding coordinating.

I had twenty-three bridesmaids in my wedding party, so if anyone can appreciate the need for a wedding coordinator (in my case, TWO of them), it's me. ;) I find no greater joy in my work than helping my couples and their families relax and just enjoy their day together.

I am married to my handsome husband, Elijah, who is so incredibly patient with me. We have been blessed with three wonderful children, and our lives have been made even sweeter by our family, friends, and local church body. 🤍
