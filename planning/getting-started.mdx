Getting started with Joy Inc. is simple. Whether your wedding is six months away or six weeks away, this page walks you through everything you need to do to book your coordinator and begin the planning process.

## Step 1 — Choose Your Package

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse Packages">
    Head to our [packages overview](/packages/overview) to explore all four coordination tiers — from the intimate Elopement Essentials to the full-scale Ultimate Celebration. Each package listing includes what's covered, how many hours of coordination are included, and a helpful estimator to guide your decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach Out">
    Once you have a sense of which package fits your wedding, email us at [joyincict@gmail.com](mailto:joyincict@gmail.com) with the subject line **"Wedding Coordination Consultation"**. In your message, share a little about your date, your approximate guest count, your venue, and your overall vision. There's no need to have everything figured out — this is just the beginning of the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book Your Consultation">
    We'll schedule a free consultation call to go over your wedding details together. This is your chance to ask questions, talk through any concerns, and confirm which package is the right fit. Your coordinator will take notes and begin getting familiar with your day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign Your Agreement">
    Once you've confirmed your package and all the details feel right, you'll receive a coordination agreement to review and sign. This formalizes your booking and reserves your coordinator for your wedding date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin Planning">
    Depending on your package, you and your coordinator will begin pre-wedding planning conversations right away. Your coordinator will start building your day-of timeline, gathering vendor information, and putting the pieces of your celebration into place.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Have Ready

You don't need to have every detail locked in before you reach out, but the more information you can share early on, the faster your coordinator can get to work. It's helpful to have the following on hand when you first connect with us:

- **Wedding date** — even a tentative date helps us check availability
- **Venue name and address** — ceremony and reception locations (these may be the same)
- **Approximate guest count** — a rough number is fine at this stage
- **Vendor list** — any vendors you've already booked (photographer, caterer, florist, DJ, officiant, etc.)
- **Ceremony and reception start times** — or your ideal timing if nothing is confirmed yet

<Tip>
  The earlier you book, the more time your coordinator has to build a thorough plan. We recommend reaching out at least 2–3 months before your wedding date.
</Tip>
