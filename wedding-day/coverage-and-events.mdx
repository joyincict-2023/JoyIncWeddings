Joy Inc. coordinates every part of your wedding day — from the first look through the final send-off. Coverage hours and events vary by package, so understanding how coverage works helps you choose the right fit for your celebration.

## Hours of Coverage by Package

Each Joy Inc. package is designed to match the scale and pace of a different kind of wedding. Here's a quick look at how coverage hours break down:

| Package | Coverage Hours | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| The Elopement Essentials | Up to 4 hours | Elopements, micro-weddings |
| The Intimate Keepsake | Up to 6 hours | Small weddings |
| The Joyful Gathering | Up to 8 hours | Traditional weddings |
| The Ultimate Celebration | 8+ hours (full day) | Large or complex weddings |

## Events Your Coordinator Can Cover

Your coordinator can be present for every major moment of your wedding day. Here's what each event looks like with Joy Inc. on your team:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="First Look & Pre-Ceremony Photos">
    Your coordinator positions you and your wedding party, cues the first look moment so it feels natural and unhurried, and keeps the photo session moving on schedule — so you arrive at your ceremony relaxed and on time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ceremony">
    Your coordinator manages processional order and timing, stays in close communication with your officiant and musicians, and handles any ceremony logistics that come up — from late arrivals to sound adjustments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cocktail Hour">
    Your coordinator oversees catering flow, guides guests through the transition, and ensures you have a few quiet minutes to decompress or sneak away for photos before the reception begins.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reception">
    Your coordinator cues your DJ or band for every entrance, first dance, toast, and cake cutting — and manages dinner service timing so the evening flows naturally from one moment to the next.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Dinner & Formalities">
    Your coordinator keeps all formalities — toasts, parent dances, bouquet toss, and any other special moments — on schedule and ensures transitions between them feel smooth and intentional, not rushed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send-off">
    Your coordinator organizes your guests for the send-off line, coordinates with your transportation provider, and makes sure your final exit is everything you envisioned — a memorable close to a perfect day.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to Decide What Coverage You Need

The right amount of coverage depends on your wedding's size, timeline, and the moments that matter most to you. A good starting point is to think through your day from start to finish and identify where you'd most want a professional by your side. For some couples, that's the full day — for others, it's the ceremony and reception. There's no wrong answer, and your coordinator is there to help you figure it out.

<Tip>
  Think about which parts of your day feel most overwhelming or unpredictable — those are often the moments where having a coordinator makes the biggest difference. Your Joy Inc. coordinator will help you map this out during your consultation.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Not sure which events apply to your wedding? During your consultation, your coordinator will walk through your vision and help you identify exactly what coverage makes sense.
</Note>
