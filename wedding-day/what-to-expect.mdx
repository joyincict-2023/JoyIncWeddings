Your wedding day should feel joyful, not stressful. When you work with Joy Inc., you wake up knowing that someone experienced is already on-site, managing every detail, so you can focus on getting ready, being with the people you love, and savoring every moment.

## Your Coordinator's Day Starts Before Yours

Before you ever step foot in your venue, your coordinator is already there. They arrive early to meet vendors as they pull in, confirm that setup is progressing according to plan, and walk through the space to catch anything that needs attention. Your master timeline is in their hands from the moment they arrive, and they're already in contact with your florist, photographer, caterer, and any other vendors — so that by the time you arrive, everything is in motion and on track.

## Your Day, Moment by Moment

Here's how a typical Joy Inc. coordinated wedding day unfolds:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Early Arrival">
    Your coordinator arrives at the venue ahead of everyone else, greets vendors as they arrive, confirms setup details, and does a full walkthrough to ensure the space matches your vision and timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pre-Ceremony">
    Your coordinator manages all first look logistics, guides your wedding party into position, and cues your photographer and musicians so every moment happens exactly when it should.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ceremony">
    Your coordinator runs the processional, keeps timing precise, and stays in close communication with your officiant and musicians to ensure the ceremony flows beautifully from start to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cocktail Hour">
    Your coordinator oversees the catering setup and flow, keeps guests informed and comfortable, and makes sure the transition from ceremony to cocktail hour is seamless.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reception">
    Your coordinator cues your DJ or band for every entrance, first dance, toast, and cake cutting moment — and manages dinner service timing so the evening feels effortless.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send-off">
    Your coordinator organizes the send-off line, coordinates your transportation, and manages final vendor departures so your exit is everything you imagined — and your last memory of the day is a great one.
  </Step>
</Steps>
