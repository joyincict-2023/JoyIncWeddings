## About the Service

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What exactly does a wedding coordinator do?">
    A wedding coordinator focuses on executing your wedding day — managing your timeline, communicating with vendors, and making sure every moment unfolds as planned. This is different from a wedding planner, who helps you build your vision from scratch over the months leading up to your wedding. Joy Inc. specializes in coordination: we make sure everything you've already planned actually happens the way you imagined it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How far in advance should I book?">
    We recommend booking at least 2–3 months before your wedding date. That said, some dates fill up quickly — especially in peak wedding season — so the sooner you reach out, the better. If your wedding is coming up soon, don't hesitate to contact us anyway; we'll do our best to accommodate you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I really need a coordinator if my venue has one?">
    Yes — and here's why: your venue coordinator's job is to manage the venue itself, not your wedding. They focus on the space, the catering staff, and the facility's needs. Your Joy Inc. coordinator focuses entirely on you — managing your vendors, your timeline, and your experience from start to finish. The two roles complement each other beautifully.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a difference between day-of coordination and full-service coordination?">
    Day-of coordination means a professional is present on your wedding day to execute everything you've already planned — managing vendors, running the timeline, and handling any surprises. Full-service coordination starts much earlier and includes helping you plan every detail from venue selection to vendor booking. Joy Inc.'s packages range from day-of focused coordination to more comprehensive support that begins weeks or months before your wedding day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add hours if my wedding runs long?">
    Yes. If you think your event may extend beyond your package's coverage hours, we encourage you to reach out in advance so we can discuss your options and make sure you have the support you need. It's always better to plan for extra time than to be caught off guard on the day itself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
